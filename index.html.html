Our Offers

How we produce —
five methods, one studio.

01
Screen Printing
Ink pushed through a mesh screen directly onto fabric. The go-to for bold, high-opacity graphics on large runs. Produces crisp, durable prints that last — ideal for hoodies, tees, and sportswear with strong block colours or striking artwork.
Best for: large runs · bold graphics · hoodies & tees
02
Embroidery
Thread stitched directly into the garment using precision machinery. Adds a premium, tactile quality that screen printing can't replicate. Perfect for crests, logos, lettering, and patches — the method of choice for varsity jackets, polos, and formal wear.
Best for: logos & crests · varsity jackets · premium feel
03
Sublimation Printing
Dye is infused into the fabric at a molecular level using heat. The result is a full-colour, edge-to-edge print that won't crack, fade, or peel. Suited for all-over patterns, complex gradients, and performance sportswear where detail and durability both matter.
Best for: all-over prints · match kits · performance wear
04
DTF Printing
Direct-to-Film transfers allow full-colour, high-detail prints to be applied to virtually any garment — regardless of colour or fabric. Great for small runs, individual personalisation, and designs that require fine lines, gradients, or photographic detail.
Best for: small runs · personalisation · photographic detail
05
Cut & Sew
Garments built entirely from scratch — pattern made, fabric cut, and sewn to your exact specification. No compromises on fit, fabric, or silhouette. The highest level of customisation we offer, used when an off-the-shelf base simply won't cut it.
Best for: fully custom silhouettes · premium collections · unique pieces
