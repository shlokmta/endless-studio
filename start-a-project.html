Design to delivery

Four steps.
One point of contact.

From the first brief to the day it lands in your hall or locker room, everything stays in-house — no handoffs, no chasing suppliers.

01
Brief & Scope

You share the sector, quantities, and timeline. We ask the right questions and set a clear scope.

02
Design

Our in-house studio turns it into on-brand, production-ready concepts — with a sample to approve.

03
Produce

Once approved, we handle materials, manufacturing, and quality control across all five methods.

04
Deliver

Individually packaged or bulk-shipped across Europe — on time and ready to use immediately.

No white-label

Built from
the fabric up.

We don't print your logo on someone else's blank. Every piece starts at the cloth and is drafted, cut, and constructed to your specification.

Most suppliers buy stock garments and decorate them. We start earlier — selecting the fabric, drafting the pattern, and building the piece before a single stitch of branding is applied.

01
Fabric Selection

We choose the cloth first — weight, hand-feel, stretch, and durability matched to how the piece will actually be worn.

02
Pattern Making

Patterns drafted in-house to our own blocks and fit — never pulled off a catalogue rail. The silhouette is yours.

03
Cut & Construction

Fabric cut and sewn to spec, with seams, trims, and finishing checked at every stage of the build.

04
Branding & Detailing

Embroidery, print, and hardware applied by the method the design calls for — the final layer, not the starting point.

05
Quality Control & Delivery

Every piece inspected, individually packaged, and delivered ready to wear — across Europe, on time.

How we produce

Five methods,
one standard.

Every project takes a different route to the same quality. Here's the quick guide to which fits yours.

01
Screen Printing

Bold, high-opacity graphics printed straight onto fabric — crisp, durable, and cost-effective at volume.

Large runs · bold graphics
hoodies & tees
02
Embroidery

Thread stitched into the garment for a premium, tactile finish print can't match. The choice for crests and lettering.

03
Sublimation Printing

Dye infused into the fabric for full-colour, edge-to-edge prints that never crack, fade, or peel.

All-over prints · match kits
performance wear
04
DTF Printing

Full-colour, high-detail transfers onto virtually any fabric or colour — ideal for short runs and personalisation.
